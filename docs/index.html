Findings

What the analysis says, how sure it is, and what the bank should do about it.

Operations

Weekend checks wait days, not hours

A check sent on Saturday waits a median of 46 hours for review, against about 9 hours on a weekday. A Mann-Whitney U test puts the gap far beyond chance (p < 0.001).

Do: add a short Saturday review shift.

Operations

Reviewers don't agree on what to deny

Denial rates run from 1.6% to 5.1% depending on the reviewer. Chi-square tests within each check size rule out chance (p < 0.001), and every reviewer sees the same mix of checks.

Do: write down denial criteria and have reviewers regularly judge the same sample of checks.

Customers

Going quiet is the warning sign

A model trained on balances, savings, denied checks and activity predicts who goes dormant well (ROC AUC 0.93), but "days since last activity" alone does just as well. 53 customers look at risk today, holding $702K.

Do: send a weekly report of customers who have gone quiet, largest balances first.

Customers

A few customers hold most of the money

The top tenth of customers hold 40% of all deposits, and the Gini coefficient is 0.55. Losing one large customer costs as much as losing dozens of typical ones.

Do: put the largest balances first in any retention work.

Growth

New customers wait a week for money to arrive

The median new customer waits 8 days for a first check to clear, and 1 in 10 waits two weeks, in every quarter. Most of that is waiting for the next paycheck.

Do: let customers fund the account when they sign up.

Method

Bank of the Future started as a PHP banking web app. This project keeps its rules and data model, simulates two years of activity, and analyzes it with the tools a data team would use.

  1. 1 · Python

    Simulate

    2,000 customers with paychecks, ATM visits, transfers and savings, following the app's rules: deposits wait for review, overdrafts are refused, interest compounds yearly.

    build.py

  2. 2 · PostgreSQL

    Store and query

    Five tables, four analysis views, and eight queries that each answer one business question, using window functions, CTEs, percentiles and a z-test.

    sql/

  3. 3 · Python notebook

    Test and predict

    pandas, scipy and scikit-learn check whether the gaps are real and build the dormancy model.

    analysis.ipynb

  4. 4 · Tableau and this site

    Show

    The same views are exported for Tableau dashboards, and this page is built from the SQL results.

    Tableau guide
